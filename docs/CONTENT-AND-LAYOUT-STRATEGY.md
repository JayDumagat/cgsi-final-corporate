# CGSI content and layout strategy

## Reference review

The redesign uses patterns observed across current large financial institutions without copying their
visual identity:

- Vanguard: simplify navigation around common investor tasks and reduce information overload.
- Fidelity Institutional: separate audience needs, solutions, research, and proof into distinct sections.
- BlackRock Institutional: give research and timely market context editorial prominence instead of
  filling the homepage with a catalogue of capabilities.

## Copywriting approach

### 1. Concrete before clever

Headlines name the user outcome or task. Avoid abstract phrases such as "clarity and perspective"
unless the next line immediately proves what they mean.

Examples:
- "A clearer way into the Philippine market."
- "Support before, during, and after the trade."
- "Research that tells you what changed—and when."

### 2. One message per section

Each major section answers one question. Supporting copy is limited to one short paragraph before
the next action.

### 3. Specific proof over adjectives

Use verifiable facts such as:
- active PSE Trading Participant;
- Broker-Dealer;
- retail and institutional client service;
- research publications available.

Avoid unsupported language such as "leading", "world-class", "best-in-class", or "trusted".

### 4. Outcome-oriented calls to action

Prefer verbs that describe what happens next:
- Open an account
- Talk to CGSI
- Read publication
- View all services
- Start with the basics
- View account requirements

Avoid vague CTAs such as "Discover more" or "Unlock possibilities".

### 5. Progressive disclosure

The homepage introduces. Destination pages explain. Service and client detail pages handle depth.
This keeps new investors from facing professional detail too early while allowing experienced users
to move directly to research and execution content.

## Layout model

1. Utility bar: regulatory/client access.
2. Primary navigation with mega menus.
3. Hero: one proposition, one image, two actions.
4. Proof bar: verifiable credentials.
5. Intent section: user self-selection.
6. Research spotlight: current editorial value.
7. Core service story: how the brokerage relationship works.
8. Client segmentation: private vs institutional needs.
9. Resource cards: education, research, official information.
10. Latest research.
11. Account-opening CTA and disclosures.
12. Footer.

## Accessibility and HCI

- 44–48px target sizes for primary interactive controls.
- visible keyboard focus.
- restrained reading measures.
- descriptive alt text for meaningful photography.
- reduced-motion, increased-contrast, and forced-colors support.
- mobile layout is recomposed rather than scaled down.
- mega menus apply progressive disclosure and recognition over recall.
