# CGSI design and product decision log

This is a compact record of decisions made during the homepage and navigation iteration. It
captures intent, not only the current code.

## Brand and positioning

### Institutional, but human

CGSI should feel like a serious brokerage firm with people behind the work. The site should not
sound like a generic corporate vendor or an intimidating financial institution. Human connection
comes from visible accountability, audience relevance, direct language, and working imagery.

### Partner rather than product seller

The website should position CGSI as a partner around investment decisions and the trade lifecycle.
Avoid product-pushing language, unsupported trust lines, exaggerated social proof, and cliche CTA
microcopy.

### No hero social-proof strip

Social-proof logos or trust-statistic lines were considered visually distracting and are not part
of the hero direction. Trust is expressed through company credentials, governance, leadership,
market information, and useful process explanations.

## Homepage structure

The working flow is:

`Hero -> About and credentials -> Our services -> Who we serve -> How clients engage -> Market snapshot and research -> Leadership -> Getting started -> Resources -> News -> Final CTA`

The sequence moves from identity, to fit, to service discovery, to process, to evidence,
accountability, and action. Quick actions remain hidden in source while the flow is refined; they
were not permanently deleted.

## Hero

- Keep the hero substantial enough to establish the firm and market context.
- Keep headline and supporting copy readable and natural rather than forcing dramatic line breaks.
- Use one dominant primary CTA: `Open an account`.
- Use a lower-priority orientation CTA such as `Market insights`.
- Do not add decorative trust lines or a crowded CTA cluster underneath the buttons.

## Services

- The section is labeled `Our services`, not `Capabilities`.
- The existing heading and supporting paragraph remain stable while the display changes.
- Categories are selected with a compact tablist rather than showing every service card at once.
- Tabs use short labels and a restrained active underline so they do not compete with the main
  navigation or look like large promotional buttons.
- The panel can show category imagery, a category explanation, and links to the relevant service
  pages.
- Categories are presentation groupings and must not be treated as final legal product taxonomy.

## Who we serve

Audience pathways should use real situations and human imagery. On mobile the audience grid becomes
one card per row. The section should feel approachable without becoming playful or consumer-app-like.

## Leadership

The leadership section should help visitors know who carries responsibility. Each current leader is
represented by an individual card with a dedicated portrait slot, name, role, and role-based
responsibility summary. Portrait slots remain intentionally blank until approved images are added;
generic people photography must not be assigned to named executives.

## Utility bar

The utility bar is a narrow service strip, not a second navigation. The current direction is:

- Language at the far left with a globe.
- Help with a life-buoy icon.
- Contact with a Lucide mail icon.
- Accessibility as a compact icon-only control.
- Theme control before a separator.
- Login after the separator.
- Color-only hover states rather than hover borders.
- Language details opening toward the bottom-left.

## Decisions still requiring source or owner

- Exact final service taxonomy and availability.
- Final account model names and definitions such as ITF, cash/regular, managed, discretionary,
  margin, DMA, and PERA variants.
- Approved executive biographies and headshots.
- Final legal/compliance review of homepage and service copy.
- Final production market snapshot data and update workflow.
- Final theme icon decision: the current compact component uses Sun in day mode and Moon in night
  mode; an earlier direction requested Star in day mode and Moon in night mode.
